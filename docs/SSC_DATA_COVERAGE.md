# SSC & one-day exam mapping content

## Included in this data expansion

The shared topic catalogue now includes five additional interactive map modules:

- National Parks of India
- Tiger Reserves of India
- Major Dams & Multipurpose Projects
- Major Rivers & River Systems
- Mountain Passes of India

Each module contains state-tagged locations, coordinates, category filters, short revision facts, and six four-option practice questions. The existing map, teaching, detective and quiz flows are reused; no separate static-only screen is introduced.

## Exam coverage intent

Designed as static-GK support for SSC CGL, CHSL, CPO, MTS, GD, Railway NTPC and similar one-day examinations. It is a topic expansion, not a claim that these are the complete syllabi of every exam. The official notification for the candidate's exam cycle remains authoritative.

## Data quality note

Coordinates are approximate learning-map markers, not survey-grade boundaries. Some pass and source-region points are representative locations. Before using a location as a high-stakes factual answer, cross-check the relevant government source and official map. Dynamic counts (for example, the number of Ramsar sites or tiger reserves) should be maintained with a dated source rather than hard-coded as timeless facts.
