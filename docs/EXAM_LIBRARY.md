# Exam Library — starter content and behaviour

## Coverage
The initial bank contains 12 topic modules and 60 four-option MCQs across:
- Indian Polity (Constitution, Parliament and constitutional bodies)
- History (Modern India, Ancient India)
- Geography (India's physical geography and climate)
- General Science (Biology, Physics, Chemistry)
- Economy
- Banking Awareness
- Computer Awareness
- Static GK

Each question includes an explanation and a reference label/URL. These are starter learning references, not a claim that every item has been independently audited against the latest official source. Dynamic policy, rates, current affairs and exam notices must be rechecked against the issuing authority.

## In-app modes
- Topic practice: instant feedback and per-module last score / attempt count.
- Flashcards: answer reveal and local spaced-revision scheduling (1, 3, 7, 14, 30 days; missed cards return after 10 minutes).
- Mock test: up to 20 randomly selected questions for the selected exam filter; score appears after submission.

## Storage and limitations
Progress and revision schedules are stored in browser localStorage on the current device/browser. There is no account sync, cross-device backup, timed mock, official PYQ attribution, or adaptive difficulty in this iteration. Clearing browser storage can erase local progress.
